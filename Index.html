Waiting on God

Christian Book
Before

Waiting is one of the hardest thing in life. Many time we pray and pray but nothing seem to be happening, and we begin to ask ourselves if God is even hearing us, but I want to tell you that God is always hearing you and he is never late.

When Joseph was in the prison he did not know that the prison was a preparation for the palace. He was there for many years, he was forgotten by the people he helped and he suffered for something he did not do, but God was with him all the time and he was working behind the scene.

So if you are waiting right now dont give up. Your season is coming and when it comes it will be worth the wait, so keep trusting and keep praying and keep believing because God has not forgotten about you

After

Waiting is one of the hardest things in life. We pray and pray, and nothing seems to move. Slowly, we start to wonder whether God is even listening. But He hears you, and He is never late.

When Joseph was in prison, he had no idea it was a preparation for a palace. He spent years there, forgotten by the people he had helped, suffering for something he didn’t do. Yet God was with him the whole time, working behind the scenes.

So if you’re waiting right now, don’t give up. Your season is coming, and it will be worth the wait. Keep trusting. Keep praying. God has not forgotten you.

What I changed: plural and verb-agreement errors (thing→things, seem→seems, scene→scenes), missing apostrophes and commas, a comma splice, a wrong word (hearing→listening), God’s pronoun capitalised, filler cut, and one long run-on split into short, strong sentences. The message and voice stayed exactly the same.

Stop Waiting for Motivation

Self-Help Book
Before

Most people thinks that motivation is what you need before you start. They waits for the perfect mood, the perfect time, and the perfect plan, but the truth is that those day never come. If you keep waiting you will wait forever.

Motivation dont come first, action does. When you take a small step, even a very small one, your mind begin to believe that change is possible and that is when the motivation start to show up. Its not the other way round.

So today, do not wait to feel ready. Pick one thing and do it for ten minutes. Just ten minutes, you will be surprised at how much you can do when you stop waiting and you start moving

After

Most people think motivation is something you need before you start. They wait for the perfect mood, the perfect time, and the perfect plan. But those days never come. If you keep waiting, you’ll wait forever.

Motivation doesn’t come first. Action does. When you take a small step, even a tiny one, your mind begins to believe change is possible. That’s when motivation shows up. It’s never the other way around.

So today, don’t wait to feel ready. Pick one thing and do it for ten minutes. Just ten. You’ll be surprised how much you can do once you stop waiting and start moving.

What I changed: subject-verb agreement (thinks→think, waits→wait, begin→begins), plural and apostrophe errors, a comma splice, and wordy phrases like “the truth is that.” Short sentences give the advice more punch.
